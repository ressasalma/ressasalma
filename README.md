## ୨୧ About Me

Hi! I'm **Ressa Salma Fatikah**, an Informatics Engineering student at **Universitas Nurdin Hamzah, Indonesia**.

I'm interested in exploring how technology, creativity, and problem-solving can come together to build something useful.

My journey started with Software Engineering (Rekayasa Perangkat Lunak) in vocational high school, where I developed an interest in web development. Since then, I've explored different areas, from building web applications and designing interfaces to editing videos and analyzing data.

I'm particularly interested in:

- Web Development & Application Development
- UI Design & Visual Creativity
- Data Analysis & Natural Language Processing
- Technology and Digital Innovation

Outside the technical side, I'm also involved in student organizations and enjoy exploring things beyond my academic field.

I don't believe I have to stick to just one interest. I'm still learning, experimenting, and discovering what I can create.

> Not lost, just collecting pieces.

## ✿ Selected Projects

A collection of academic projects, experiments, and ideas I've worked on or explored.

### 🌐 Personal Portfolio

A personal website to introduce myself, showcase my projects, skills, experiences, and creative works.

`React` · `Vite` 

[Explore Repository](https://github.com/ressasalma/portofolio-ressa)

### 📢 School Facilities Complaint Application

A web-based application that allows students to submit complaints and suggestions regarding school facilities, while administrators can manage reports and provide feedback.

Built with PHP and MySQL, featuring student and administrator roles, complaint history, status tracking, and report filtering.

`PHP` · `MySQL` · `HTML` · `CSS` · `JavaScript`

### 💬 Indonesian Cyberbullying Analysis

An academic data analysis project exploring Indonesian-language social media comments using Python.

The project focuses on text preprocessing, dataset exploration, and identifying cyberbullying patterns through data analysis.

`Python` · `Pandas` · `Jupyter Notebook` · `NLP`

### 🏠 JeJakos — Boarding House Platform

A concept for a digital platform that helps people find boarding houses in Jambi based on location, budget, and facilities.

The idea includes property discovery, maps, chatbot assistance, booking, and tenant-owner communication.

*Project concept / development exploration.*

### 📄 RapiInDoc — Academic Document Formatter

An idea for a document formatting platform that simplifies the organization of academic papers, reports, and proposals.

The concept focuses on automatic formatting of covers, headings, page numbering, and document structure based on reference templates.

*Project concept / development exploration.*

### 🔎 Temu.in — Campus Lost & Found

A concept for a campus lost-and-found platform designed to help people reconnect with their missing belongings through location-based discovery and item status tracking.

*Project concept / design exploration.*
